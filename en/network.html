How to read this web

This web is drawn not as a family tree descending from top to bottom but as ripples spreading from a centre. With Dongju at the centre, each new generation adds a ring further out. A later generation does not stand below an earlier one; it stands on a wider circle.

The teaching ties running outward from the centre are the backbone of this tradition, but not the whole of it. Counted up, the peer ties running along each ring and the collaboration ties crossing between rings are more numerous. Reading and writing alongside contemporaries, and working jointly across generations, have sustained this tradition as much as learning from a teacher.

Citation ties arise even between those who never met: they are the lines reaching straight from the centre to an outer ring. Repeatedly citing the writings of an earlier generation is a way of inheriting without being taught directly, and it is also the broadest evidentiary basis on which this archive judges relations.
