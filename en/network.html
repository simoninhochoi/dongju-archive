How to read this web

The vertical teaching ties are the backbone of this tradition, but not the whole of it. Counted up, the horizontal collaboration and peer ties are more numerous. Reading and writing alongside contemporaries, and working jointly across generations, have sustained this tradition as much as learning from a teacher.

Citation ties arise even between those who never met. Repeatedly citing the writings of an earlier generation is a way of inheriting without being taught directly, and it is also the broadest evidentiary basis on which this archive judges relations.
