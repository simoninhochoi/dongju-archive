Scholars

Fifteen scholars across five generations · each entry opens with a single paragraph on that scholar's guiding question

First generationb. 1917
Lee Yong-hee李用熙
1917–1997 · Seoul National University · pen name Lee Dong-ju
Sought to establish international politics not as a science of universal laws but as an inquiry made from where one stands. Questioned early on the claim of Western IR to universalise the experience of its birthplace, and pressed that question through the concept of the sphere (kwŏnyŏk).
sphereplacetransmission
120 works  ·  1940–2018  ·  EN 2 · JA 14
Second generationb. 1930s
Noh Jae-bong盧在鳳
b. 1936 · Seoul National University
Attempted an intellectual-historical self-understanding of Korean IR, pursuing at once an inquiry into the political conditions of scholarship and a rare path of direct political engagement.
intellectual historystate
88 works  ·  1969–2021  ·  EN 5 · JA 11
Kim Yong-koo金容九
1937–2025 · Seoul National University · Hallym
Treated the passage of the modern international order into East Asia as a problem of translation and concepts. By tracing the Chinese rendering and reception of international law, showed that it arrived not as universal but as translated.
international lawtranslationconcepts
121 works  ·  1964–2021  ·  EN 3 · JA 21
Third generationb. 1940s
Ha Young-sun河英善
b. 1947 · Seoul National University · East Asia Institute
Carried Dongju's questions forward in the languages of conceptual history and complexity. Traced how modern Korea imported concepts such as civilisation, state, and nation, and built a framework for reading twenty-first-century order as compound.
conceptual historycomplexity
320 works  ·  1974–2024  ·  EN 22 · JA 14 · ZH 1
Fourth generationb. 1960s
Chun Chaesung全在成
b. 1966 · Seoul National University
Reopened the organising principle of international politics as a theoretical question. Doubted anarchy as a single premise, proposed hierarchy and compound organising principles, and addressed postcolonial theory alongside Korea's position.
organising principlehierarchypostcolonial
254 works  ·  1987–2026  ·  EN 43 · JA 3
